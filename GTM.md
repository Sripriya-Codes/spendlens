# Go-To-Market Plan

## Exact target user

Engineering Manager or CTO at a 5–30 person startup, Series A or bootstrapped, currently paying for 3+ AI tools across their team with no centralised tracking. They approved the tools ad-hoc and now pay the bill monthly without questioning it. They're not cheap — they're just busy and have no benchmark.

## What they Google before wanting this

- "cursor vs github copilot for teams"
- "is claude pro worth it"
- "AI tools for dev teams cost"
- "reduce SaaS spend startup"

## Where they hang out

- r/ExperiencedDevs, r/startups, r/SaaS on Reddit
- Hacker News (Show HN and Ask HN)
- Indie Hackers community and forum
- Twitter/X — following @levelsio, @paulg, @benedictevans
- Slack: Rands Leadership, SaaStr community

## First 100 users in 30 days, $0 budget

1. **Show HN post** — "Show HN: I built a free audit tool for AI tool overspend" — HN readers are exactly this audience. One good Show HN can drive 500+ visits.
2. **Post in r/ExperiencedDevs** — frame it as "built this after noticing we were paying for 4 overlapping AI tools" — personal story performs better than product pitches.
3. **Cold DM 20 indie hackers on Twitter** — find people who've tweeted about their AI tool stack in the last 30 days. "Built something you might find useful — would love honest feedback."
4. **Indie Hackers forum post** — "What I learned auditing AI spend at 10 startups" — content-first, tool linked at the end.
5. **Share the shareable audit URL from real audits** — run audits for 5 friends who'll share their results on Twitter. The share card is designed for this.

## Unfair distribution channel

Credex already has relationships with companies that buy AI credits. Every existing Credex customer is a warm lead for SpendLens — they already care about AI spend. A single email to the Credex customer list could drive 200+ audits on day one.

## Week-1 traction if this works

- 300+ audits completed
- 80+ emails captured
- 3+ audit result URLs shared organically on Twitter
- 1 Credex consultation booked from the high-savings CTA
