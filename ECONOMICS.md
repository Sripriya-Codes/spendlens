# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assume average deal: $2,000 in credits at 25% margin = $500 gross profit per customer. Assume 12-month LTV with 60% retention = ~$800 LTV per converted customer.

## CAC by channel

| Channel           | Estimated CAC                 |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Show HN / organic | ~$0 (time only)               |
| Cold DM outreach  | ~$5 (time cost)               |
| Credex email list | ~$0 (already owned)           |
| Paid Twitter      | ~$80–120 (not used in week 1) |

## Conversion funnel

- Audit completed → email captured: 30% (email shown after value, not before)
- Email captured → Credex CTA shown (>$500 savings): 20% of leads
- Credex CTA → consultation booked: 15%
- Consultation → credit purchase: 40%

Overall: 100 audits → 30 emails → 6 see Credex CTA → 0.9 consultations → 0.36 purchases

At $800 LTV: 100 audits = ~$288 revenue. At $0 CAC (organic), this is pure margin.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

- Need: 1,250 paying customers at $800 LTV
- Requires: ~350,000 audits completed (at 0.36% audit→purchase rate)
- Monthly audits needed: ~20,000/month by month 12
- This is achievable if SpendLens gets one viral Show HN moment (typically 5,000–15,000 visits) and compounds via shared audit URLs

What must be true:

1. Share URL gets clicked by 20% of people who receive it
2. Credex closes 40%+ of consultations (strong sales team needed)
3. Pricing data stays current — tool loses trust fast if numbers are wrong
